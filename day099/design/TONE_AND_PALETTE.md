# day099 Tone and Palette

## LP Premise

- Theme: fictional landing page for bouldering gear.
- Goal: drive purchase intent for a starter gear set and individual products.
- Audience: beginner to intermediate bouldering users who want better grip, safer preparation, and gear that looks sharp.
- Brand handling: do not show manufacturer logos, brand marks, or readable maker names in images or UI copy.

## Tone

- Core mood: black-based cyberpunk, high contrast, sharp, technical, energetic.
- Trust level: practical product detail and realistic photography keep the page credible.
- Warmth: keep the copy direct and motivating, not cold or overly industrial.
- Visual keywords: neon rim light, carbon texture, chalk dust, climbing hold texture, laser grid, glass panels, numeric specs.
- Avoid: fantasy sci-fi, toy-like 3D, fake holograms that hide the product, unreadable neon overload.

## Color Palette

| Role | Hex | Usage |
|---|---:|---|
| Deep Black | `#05060A` | Page base, hero background |
| Graphite Surface | `#0B0F17` | Section backgrounds |
| Carbon Panel | `#111827` | Product cards, spec blocks |

| Primary Neon Cyan | `#00E5FF` | Focus states, key highlights, glow accents |
| Electric Magenta | `#FF2BD6` | Secondary accents, hover glow, visual rhythm |

| Text White | `#F5F7FA` | Main copy |
| Mist Gray | `#A6B0C3` | Supporting text |
| Muted Slate | `#667085` | Captions, metadata |

## Typography Direction

- Japanese UI: modern sans serif with clean strokes, such as `Noto Sans JP` or system sans.
- Numeric/spec labels: compact techno feel, such as `Rajdhani`, `Barlow Condensed`, or a similar fallback.
- Headlines: short, hard-edged phrases with strong verbs.
- Body copy: concise and purchase-oriented; avoid long storytelling.

## Photography and Asset Rules

- Product images should be realistic photo style.
- Use black studio backgrounds, neon cyan/magenta rim lighting, shallow depth of field, visible chalk particles, textured rubber, nylon, brush bristles, and climbing holds.
- Products must be logo-free and label-free. If packaging appears, keep it plain or unreadable.
- Suggested product subjects: liquid chalk bottle, chalk bag, brush, finger tape, grip balm, compact starter kit.
- Avoid: readable brand text, existing maker logos, cartoon style, glossy unrealistic plastic, excessive blur.

## CTA Style

- Primary CTA: solid `#FF5A1F`, dark text or white text depending on contrast, with cyan glow on hover.
- Secondary CTA: transparent black panel with `#00E5FF` border.
- CTA copy candidates:
  - `スターターセットを購入する`
  - `今すぐギアを選ぶ`
  - `グリップ性能を見る`
- CTA placement: hero first viewport, sticky mobile bottom CTA, product card CTA, final purchase band.

## Section Rhythm

1. Hero: realistic product photo, sharp headline, primary purchase CTA.
2. Problem: sweat, chalk loss, brush wear, gear scatter.
3. Product lineup: logo-free products with specs and price-like anchors.
4. Performance proof: grip, carry, maintenance, beginner readiness.
5. Bundle CTA: starter kit purchase push.
6. FAQ: size, care, first purchase concerns.
7. Final CTA: short purchase-focused close.

## First Mockup Direction

Create a dark cyberpunk product LP with a large photo-realistic gear set in the hero. The page should feel like an ecommerce purchase page, not a brand manifesto. Keep all products and packaging free of logos, maker names, and readable labels.
