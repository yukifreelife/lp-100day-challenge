# Tone and Manner

## Concept

YADO Review Lab is a consulting-style LP for owners of rural renovated kominka minpaku. The service improves reservation-page clarity and guest experience before and after booking.

## Palette

- Paper White: `#FAFAF7`
- Porcelain Gray: `#E8ECEF`
- Indigo Ink: `#1C274C`
- Guest Blue: `#315C9E`
- Stamp Vermilion: `#D94E35`
- Key Brass: `#A77A2D`
- Soft Blush: `#F3D8D2`

Avoid teal, mint, sage green, sand beige, copper-led accents, lavender, and purple gradients.

## Typography

Use a refined Japanese serif feel for large headings and a readable Japanese sans-serif for UI/body text. Keep letter spacing at `0` and avoid viewport-width font scaling.

## Visual Motifs

- Thin rule lines and report-like layouts.
- Reservation-page card, rating card, check-in card, diagnosis chart.
- Brass key cutout.
- Vermilion stamp accents for "改善" and "対応外".
- Kominka room photography with natural light.

## CTA Tone

Direct and practical:

- 無料でレビュー課題を診断する
- 改善事例を見る
- 古民家民泊の改善ポイントを無料で確認する
