## Style Guidelines for AI Generation

- **Color Palette**: Warm browns (#6f4e37), beiges (#c4a484), cream tones
- **Lighting**: Warm, natural, golden hour preferred
- **Mood**: Artisanal, craft-focused, cozy, professional
- **Quality**: High-end photography, sharp details, 8K resolution preferred
- **Composition**: Clean, minimalist, focus on subject
