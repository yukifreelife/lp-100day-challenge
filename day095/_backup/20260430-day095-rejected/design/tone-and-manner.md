# MiraSkill AI Tone and Manner

## Brand Premise

MiraSkill AI is a fictional AI reskilling service for working adults. The page focuses on a free AI aptitude diagnosis that connects the visitor to a practical 12-week learning roadmap.

## Audience

- Working adults who want to use AI in current work, career change, or side work.
- Non-engineers are included, so copy must avoid intimidating technical phrasing.
- The user should feel that AI is a practical work tool, not a futuristic toy.

## Visual Direction

- Base: white and soft cloud backgrounds.
- Main text: ink black for business credibility.
- Trust color: practical green.
- Accent: warm amber for motivation and restrained cyan for AI/data cues.
- Avoid: dominant purple/blue gradients, flashy neon, stock-photo atmosphere, and overly decorative hero layouts.

## Section Rhythm

1. Hero with free diagnosis CTA and dashboard visual.
2. Problem section for decision fatigue and poor transfer to work.
3. Interactive diagnosis form with result preview.
4. 12-week roadmap.
5. Course/pricing comparison.
6. Results and learner voices.
7. FAQ and final CTA.

## CTA Strategy

- Primary: `無料AI適性診断を受ける`
- Secondary: `学習ロードマップを見る`
- Form result text should explain that the current implementation is a demo and can be connected to mail, CRM, and GA4 in production.
