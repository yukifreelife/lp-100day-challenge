# Day097 Tone and Manner

## LP Premise

サービス名案: たのめる秘書室

個人事業主や小規模チームが、日々の事務・予約調整・メール返信・資料整理を気軽に頼めるオンライン秘書サービスのLP。堅い業務委託サービスではなく、「ちょっと手が足りないときに、すぐ相談できる相棒」として見せる。

## Target User

- ひとりで仕事を抱えがちな個人事業主
- 問い合わせ対応や日程調整に時間を取られているフリーランス
- 採用するほどではないが、事務作業を任せる相手が欲しい小規模チーム
- 秘書サービスに興味はあるが、契約や依頼のハードルが高そうだと感じている人

## Desired Impression

- 親しみやすい
- 気軽に相談できる
- ていねいで任せやすい
- 明るく前向き
- 仕事の質はちゃんとしている

Avoid: 高級秘書風、法人コンサル風、冷たいSaaS風、過度にポップな便利屋感。

## Palette Rules

- Orange is the lead color, but the page base should stay warm white.
- Use `Friendly Orange` for one primary CTA per view, not every button.
- Use `Mint Note` as a balancing accent for reassurance blocks such as "依頼後の流れ" and "対応完了".
- Avoid brown-heavy shadows and coffee-like tones; keep orange clean and bright.
- Keep text contrast high by pairing orange surfaces with `Desk Ink` or white text only where contrast is sufficient.

## Typography

- Headings: rounded, approachable Japanese sans-serif feel.
- Body: highly readable Japanese sans-serif.
- Recommended stack: `"Noto Sans JP", "Hiragino Kaku Gothic ProN", "Yu Gothic", sans-serif`.
- Letter spacing: `0`.
- Heading weight should be confident but not heavy; prefer `700` for major headings and `600` for card titles.

## Copy Tone

Voice: "頼れるけれど、距離が近い".

- Use short, concrete phrases.
- Speak to the user's overloaded day, not abstract productivity.
- Make the first action feel small: "まずは相談", "1件だけお願い", "今週だけ頼む".
- Avoid stiff words such as "業務効率化ソリューション", "戦略的バックオフィス支援".

Example CTA:

- まずは15分だけ相談する
- 今週の事務をお願いしてみる
- 頼める内容を見る
- 空き状況を確認する

Example headline direction:

- その事務作業、今日からひとりで抱えなくて大丈夫。
- ちょっと頼めるオンライン秘書が、毎日の仕事を軽くします。

## UI Direction

- Layout: clean LP structure with generous breathing room, but avoid a formal corporate feel.
- First view: friendly human support and actual task examples should be visible immediately.
- Cards: white or soft cream surfaces, 8px border radius, thin warm borders, very light shadow.
- CTA: rounded 8px button, orange fill, simple arrow or calendar icon.
- Forms: low-pressure wording; show small helper text like "相談だけでも大丈夫です".
- Icons: line icons with small orange/apricot fills; use familiar symbols such as calendar, mail, checklist, file, chat.
- Proof elements: task examples, response time, supported tools, client quotes, before/after weekly schedule.
- Photography or illustration: bright desk scene, friendly remote assistant, checklist UI, soft natural light. Avoid dark corporate stock photos.

## Section Rhythm

1. Hero: "抱え込まなくていい" emotional hook plus light task chips.
2. Pain points: small daily tasks that quietly steal time.
3. Service menu: schedule, email, document, research, booking, reminders.
4. How it works: consult, assign, progress check, completion.
5. Trust: support quality, response rule, security basics, testimonials.
6. Pricing: starter-friendly plan and weekly support plan.
7. FAQ: "1件だけでも頼める？", "急ぎ対応は？", "どこまで任せられる？"
8. CTA: low-pressure consultation.

## Interaction Notes

- Keep animations light: small fade-up, task chips sliding in, CTA hover lift.
- Do not use bouncing or overly playful motion.
- Mobile first: CTA should remain obvious, but avoid sticky elements covering content.
- Buttons and form controls should feel like simple actions, not enterprise workflows.
